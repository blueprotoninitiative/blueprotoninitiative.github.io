Who we are

A collective of high school
researchers building real technology,
right now.

Blue Proton Initiative is an informal team from Forlì, Italy, doing independent research and building technology together — united by a shared passion for science and engineering. The initiative grew from a single idea Pietro had, which he brought to life by reaching out to friends who shared his vision.

We do not wait to be ready. We build, learn, and iterate — while still in high school. Each member drives both shared research and a specific technical domain, working toward one long-term goal: turning Blue Proton from a student group into a real company.

01

AI & Neural Networks

Custom YOLO architectures trained in-house for edge computing. Real-world applicability, zero cloud dependency. Expanding into biomedics and open-source research.

02

Engineering & CAD

From structural design to embedded systems — everything bridging abstract research and physical hardware. Designed and built in-house, including custom 3D printed enclosures.

03

Software & Security

Proprietary software developed entirely in-house, with a strong focus on efficiency, reliability, and cybersecurity. Lean, fast, and built to last.
